Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the registration fee?

Your registration includes access to all keynote sessions, breakout sessions, networking events, conference materials, and meals during the event.

Can I get a refund if I can't attend?

We offer full refunds up to 30 days before the event. After that, we can transfer your registration to a colleague or credit it toward a future event.

Is there a group discount?

Yes, we offer group discounts for registrations of 5 or more people from the same organization. Contact us for more information.

What COVID-19 safety measures are in place?

We follow all local health guidelines and will implement appropriate safety measures. Specific protocols will be communicated to registered attendees closer to the event date.
